1. WHAT BROKE: Amounts.AMOUNT regex strictly required two decimal places (\.[0-9]{2}), causing integer amounts like "Rs.5" to be bypassed and capturing the subsequent available balance ("Rs.92,213.10") instead.
2. HOW WE FOUND IT: Isolated via incident/app.log trace on message m-00004-9c11ae where Rs.5 was extracted as 92213.10, triggering the -1,254,130.19 balance divergence alert on account **4821.
3. WHO WAS AFFECTED: 44 messages across HDFC and ICICI accounts containing integer rupee values without decimal fractions were corrupted in the ledger.
4. FIX APPLIED: Updated AMOUNT and BALANCE regex to (?:Rs\.?|INR)\s*([0-9,]+(?:\.[0-9]{2})?) with exact 2-decimal BigDecimal scaling and backfilled affected records.
5. WHY IT CANNOT RECUR: Added regression tests in AmountsTest asserting integer amounts, thousand-separated amounts, and balance extraction; CI fails if integer amounts are skipped.
